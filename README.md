# The Twenty-Fourth — what your telescope would have seen

For Ajax and Shaz, married 24 October 2026.

Spin the wheel. Every year from 1926 to 2076, it shows what was up in the sky over Eastern Canada at nine o'clock on the night of 24 October: the moon, the brightest thing up there, stars and what their names mean, how old their light was, the odd comet. Worked out with real astronomy, not invented.

Two of the years have a pub buried in them. Go looking.

The years after 2026 are yours to write in.

**Open it:** https://rowannarepo.github.io/twenty-fourth/

## Putting it on your phone

It works like an app once it's on your home screen, and then it works with no signal.

- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap the three dots, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

## The two buttons

When you land on a year after 2026, there's a box to write what happened that 24 October. It saves on your phone by itself.

- **keep** saves everything you've written into one small file. Do it now and then, so it's safe if you ever change phones.
- **bring back** loads that file in again on a new phone.

## For Ajax

**[for-ajax.txt](https://rowannarepo.github.io/twenty-fourth/for-ajax.txt)** is the whole wheel written out plain, every year, so you can pick one any way you like and read her what was up that night.
